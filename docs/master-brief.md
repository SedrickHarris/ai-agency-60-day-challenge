# 2027 AI Agency 60-Day Challenge

**Status:** Working brief  
**Purpose:** Source of truth for the challenge concept, core rules, visual direction, and initial website scope.

## Overview

The **2027 AI Agency 60-Day Challenge** is a free AI agency course combined with a shared 60-day competition.

Participants follow Carson’s free AI agency course, use it to acquire new agency clients, submit proof of payments received, and compete on a public leaderboard.

The three eligible participants with the highest total **verified cash collected** during the shared challenge window win prizes.

> Learn the system. Land clients. Submit proof. Move up.  
> The highest verified cash collected wins.

> Public competition. Private proof. Verified winners.

## How it works

1. Participant joins through Carson’s GoHighLevel affiliate link.
2. Participant joins Carson’s Skool community.
3. Participant creates and verifies a challenge account.
4. Participant follows the free course and lands new agency clients.
5. Participant submits client and payment proof.
6. Carson’s team reviews each submission.
7. Approved payments increase verified cash collected.
8. Eligible active participants are ranked publicly from highest to lowest.

## Eligibility

Participants must:

- Be active under Carson’s GoHighLevel affiliate link
- Join Carson’s Skool community
- Have collected under $10,000 during the 30 days before challenge launch
- Enter only once per agency
- Complete verification and agree to challenge rules
- Remain eligible throughout the challenge

New, existing, and reactivated GoHighLevel customers may participate if they meet all requirements.

## What counts

Only actual payments received from eligible new agency clients count.

Eligible payments can include:

- Setup fees
- Initial service payments
- Recurring service payments
- Additional payments from the same approved client

Every approved payment must:

- Come from an eligible new client acquired after the participant joins
- Be received within the shared 60-day challenge window
- Be received while the participant is active and eligible
- Be supported by required evidence
- Be approved by Carson’s team
- Exclude taxes and client ad spend

Every new client submission requires:

- Fathom sales-call recording
- Paid invoice or payment confirmation
- Payment amount and received date
- Service description
- Taxes/ad-spend exclusion information
- Participant certification that the information is accurate

Additional payments must attach to the same client record.

## What does not count

Do not count:

- Existing clients
- Clients acquired before challenge enrollment
- Self-payments
- Related-party payments
- Duplicate clients
- Shared-agency double entries
- Unpaid contracts
- Unpaid invoices
- Free trials
- Projected revenue
- Estimated contract value
- Taxes
- Client ad spend
- Payments received while inactive
- Payments without required evidence
- Payments outside the official challenge window
- Refunded, reversed, charged-back, or invalid payments

## Ranking

Participants rank from highest to lowest by total approved verified cash collected.

```text
Verified cash collected =
Approved eligible payments
− taxes
− client ad spend
```

Only approved payments that are within the challenge window, linked to eligible clients, supported by evidence, and received during active eligibility can count.

Recommended tie-break order:

1. Highest verified cash collected
2. Earliest timestamp at which the participant reached the final tied total, based on payment-received time
3. Highest number of approved eligible new clients
4. Documented random draw among tied eligible finalists

Do not use reviewer approval timestamp as a primary tie-breaker.

## Access and reactivation

If a participant’s eligible GoHighLevel access ends:

- Remove or block Skool access where possible
- Remove or block submission access
- Hide their public leaderboard entry
- Preserve internal records
- Do not count payments received while inactive

If they reactivate through Carson’s link within 72 hours:

- Restore access
- Restore previously approved totals
- Restore public leaderboard listing if appropriate
- Do not count payments received while inactive

If they do not reactivate within 72 hours, they are disqualified.

## Copy rules

Use:

- Verified cash collected
- Eligible new-client payments
- Evidence-backed rankings
- Public standings
- Private proof
- Shared 60-day deadline
- Real client acquisition

Avoid:

- Guaranteed income
- Guaranteed clients
- Easy money
- Make money fast
- Everyone will win
- Earn $10,000 in 60 days
- Clients provided
- Student earnings

Always make clear that participants acquire their own clients.
