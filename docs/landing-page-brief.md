# Landing Page Brief

**Project:** 2027 AI Agency 60-Day Challenge  
**Purpose:** Build a premium, conversion-focused landing page using mock data only.

## Primary goal

Convert qualified visitors into challenge applicants.

The main conversion path is:

1. Visitor understands the challenge
2. Visitor confirms they are likely eligible
3. Visitor clicks Join the Challenge
4. Future flow routes them through GoHighLevel, Skool, and account verification

For the initial build, all CTAs can use placeholder links or temporary anchors.

## Brand direction

The landing page should feel:

- Premium
- Competitive
- High-stakes
- Data-driven
- Credible
- Clean
- Fast
- Mobile-first

Use:

- Near-black background
- Electric-blue glows
- White typography
- Dark cards
- Large dollar figures
- Prize-focused visuals
- Strong rankings
- High-contrast CTA buttons

Avoid purple accents. Use blue, black, white, and restrained cool gray.

## Required copy rules

Use:

- Verified cash collected
- Eligible new-client payments
- Evidence-backed rankings
- Public standings
- Private proof
- Real client acquisition

Avoid:

- Guaranteed income
- Guaranteed clients
- Easy money
- Make money fast
- Everyone wins
- Clients provided
- Earn $10,000 in 60 days

## Required disclaimer

Use this statement where appropriate:

> Results vary based on execution, offer quality, sales ability, market conditions, client demand, and other factors. Participation does not guarantee client acquisition, revenue, profit, placement, or prizes.
